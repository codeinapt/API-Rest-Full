## Evaluation of the implementer's choices
- **Row models:** they are `internal sealed`, use `init` properties, and live only in `Persistence/Read/Models`. The configurations are in `Read/Configurations` and are loaded through `ApplyConfigurationsFromAssembly`. This is clean and keeps Domain out of T-06.
- **`SystemClock` in `Infrastructure/Common`:** acceptable, because it is an infrastructure implementation of an Application port. It is registered as a singleton.
- **`IUnitOfWork` with only Begin and Commit:** matches the SEQ flow. Rollback comes from disposing the transaction, and `UnitOfWork` is registered as scoped, so the DI container disposes it at the end of the request. The `Connection` and `Transaction` accessors are exposed only on the concrete internal class, for the future Dapper repositories.
- **`SaveChanges` overrides:** the two overrides throw. The sync `SaveChanges()` and the async `SaveChangesAsync(CancellationToken)` both call the overridden two-argument methods, so all four entry points are blocked.
- **`UtcDateTimeConverter`:** it is the right approach for reading DATETIME2 values as UTC.
