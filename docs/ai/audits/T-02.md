## SPEC details
- **Matches docs/ai/10 and DOMAIN_LOGIC §4:**
  - The realm is `inventory`.
  - `inventory-api` is bearer-only, and the audience mapper `inventory-api` sits on `inventory-swagger`.
  - `inventory-swagger` is a public client with the standard flow only, and implicit, direct-grant and service accounts are off. `pkce.code.challenge.method` is `S256`, and the redirect URI is exactly `http://localhost:8080/swagger/oauth2-redirect.html`.
  - The client scopes `inventory.read` and `inventory.write` are optional on the Swagger client.
  - The realm roles are `operator`, `supervisor` and `admin`.
  - The groups are `/areas/FOOD`, `/areas/CLEAN` and `/areas/TOOLS`.
  - The `areas` mapper is `oidc-group-membership-mapper` with `full.path=false`, which gives the last path segment. This is the behaviour the spec defines.
  - The `roles` scope maps realm roles to `realm_access.roles`, which is what `KeycloakRolesClaimsTransformation` (T-03) expects.
  - The users, roles and areas match the table: `operator1`, `supervisor.food` (FOOD), `supervisor.tools` (TOOLS) and `admin1`. Operator and admin have no group, so they get no `areas` claim, and the spec says that is treated as an empty list.
- **Role-to-permission mapping (§4):** this lives in the API policies (T-03), not in Keycloak. The realm only supplies the three roles and the area groups, so there is no conflict with the matrix.
- **Verified token** for `supervisor.food`:
